# Decisions

### 2026-08-11 — Freeze pre-production at Core Loop Proof

Pre-production remains frozen at **Core Loop Proof** until
`docs/design-tests/CORE_LOOP_PROOF.md` is resolved. M4's implementation
and M5's accepted sequencing debt do not prove player desire; expanding the
unproven loop would multiply content cost before resolving its premise.

supersedes: docs/SCOPE.md §3 milestone momentum where it implies later completed work authorizes M6

### 2026-08-11 — Valenfeld owns shared world canon

Awakening is a prologue leaf: shared world roots remain in the sibling
Valenfeld typed lore graph, while `docs/CANON_BRIDGE.md` records dependencies
and collisions for this game's reveal layer.

supersedes: any reading of docs/GDD.md as authority to retcon shared Valenfeld entities

### 2026-08-11 — Use MDA as the design lens

Mechanics are chosen to produce observable dynamics in service of intended
aesthetics; hypothesis and evidence artifacts remain plain design tests, not a
new framework.

supersedes: the Measurable Design Record terminology introduced on 2026-08-11
