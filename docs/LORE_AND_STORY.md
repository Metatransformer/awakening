# Lore and story

**Awakening is a prologue.** Its story succeeds when the player wants the larger
world of Valenfeld, not when the prologue explains that world.

## Story jobs at core-loop proof

The prototype's story has four jobs:

1. Orient the player.
2. Motivate descent and return.
3. Make permanent change in the garrison feel meaningful.
4. Seed one compelling unanswered promise about the larger world.

Story work that serves none of these jobs waits.

## Canon and reveal

| Layer | Meaning | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| World canon | Frozen, typed truth about the larger Valenfeld setting. | The sibling Valenfeld lore graph, starting at [`../../valenfeld/world/LORE_INDEX.md`](../../valenfeld/world/LORE_INDEX.md). |
| Prologue canon | Truth Awakening commits to. | `GDD.md`, `SCOPE.md` and the relevant system document. |
| Current reveal | The small subset this stage needs the player to encounter. | The active MDR and approved player-facing artifact. |
| Player-facing delivery | What the player can actually see, hear or read. | The implemented scene, environment, item text or existing story beat. |

Do not collapse these layers. A world fact does not need to be revealed, and a
tentative reveal is not canon merely because it appears in a test draft.

[`CANON_BRIDGE.md`](CANON_BRIDGE.md) records the shared entity ids and any place
where the prologue currently collides with its parent canon. Do not resolve a
collision by quietly rewriting either side.

## Reveal budget

Build depth in the parent canon and reveal little here. During core-loop proof,
do not expand the reveal budget already frozen by `SCOPE.md` §6. Link to that
owner instead of copying its noun list. No lore encyclopedia work is authorised.

Hand-author the mythic and story spine. Environmental details and item text are
leaf artifacts: they cite the canon graph and serve that spine; they do not
generate a competing one. Every proposed lore beat must answer:

- What does the player understand?
- What does the player feel?
- What do they do differently?

If the answers do not affect one of the four story jobs, defer the beat.

## Cold-playtest questions

Ask after observation, without supplying the nouns:

- Where are you, and what are you doing here?
- Why did you go down? Why did you return?
- What changed after you came back, if anything?
- What do you think lies beyond this prologue?
- Which detail, if any, would you want explained later?

Promote tentative lore to canon only when it serves a story job, stays within
the scope and noun ceilings, does not contradict an existing owner, and survives
the relevant comprehension test. Record the decision in an MDR, then update the
single owning canon document. Do not promote a detail merely because it sounds
good in isolation.

If a detail changes shared world truth, promote or retcon it in the Valenfeld
canon first, then update `CANON_BRIDGE.md`. If it is prologue-only, record that
boundary explicitly so a later Valenfeld author does not mistake it for a root.
